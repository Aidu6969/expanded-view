| 8 | That air gets squeezed until it's hot | W: compression heats the mixture "to the ignition point of diesel fuel". S: "the heat generated by the compression of air". |

## Unsure items and how they were resolved
- Where the fuel goes: W says "ball pan of the impact block", S "top of the impact block", F "combustion chamber beneath the ram". Script says "onto the impact block" (W + S agree); the animation shows it landing in a bowl on top of the block.
- Exact moment of ignition: sources agree it is at/just after the ram strikes the block. The animation pauses on the block for the explanation (labelled ANIMATION SLOWED).
- No numbers stated (ram weights, blow rates, stroke heights): the three sources do not give them for diesel hammers, so the script avoids them. The blow counter on screen just counts the animated blows.
- Hook title uses "pile driver" (the searched phrase); the narration says "diesel pile driver" and the end card "diesel hammer" — both names are used by W.

## Misconceptions to avoid
- It is not a spark-ignition engine and has no crankshaft or connecting rod.
- The crane does not lift the ram each blow (that is a drop hammer); it only starts the diesel hammer.
- Shown: single-acting (open-top) hammer. Double-acting (closed-top) diesel hammers add a bounce chamber (F) — not shown, not claimed.

## Simplifications made on screen
- Animation slowed for the narrated blow; free-running blows elsewhere are stylised (no real blow rate claimed).
- Pile set per blow exaggerated so it is visible; parts not to scale; pump and lever schematic; trip shown as a simple claw on the crane line.
- Recoil dampener, hammer cushion and striker plate (F figure 9) omitted.
