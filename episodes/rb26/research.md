On screen (not narrated): iron block / aluminium head (WP: "engine block is made from cast iron, while the cylinder
head is made from aluminium"; DS, JB agree); "280 PS / 276 HP"; "2,568 cc x 1.7 = 4,366 cc"; firing order.

Unsure / resolved:
- Turbo model: WP text says T25-type, its infobox says T28. Not stated in the video.
- Drifted contradicts itself on block material (aluminium vs cast iron); WP, DS and JB all say cast iron. Not narrated.
- "Around 600 hp in race trim" is common on forums but the only sourced figure is 550 PS (Nissan Heritage); used 550.
- "Godzilla" nickname origin (Australian Wheels magazine) is popular but not confirmed in sources read: left out.
- 29 of 29: Nissan's own heritage page; phrased as "Group A races it entered in Japan".
- Animation: cars are generic illustrations (no badges or logos); engine drawing is simplified (compressor plumbing
  omitted) and the cycle is slowed.

Misconceptions avoided: the RB26 turbos are parallel, not sequential (unlike the 2JZ-GTE); the 276 hp limit was an
informal agreement, not a law.
