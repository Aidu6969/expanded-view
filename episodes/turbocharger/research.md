| 8 | A wastegate bypasses exhaust around the turbine to control boost | Garrett ("a Wastegate provides us a means to control the boost pressure"); Wikipedia |

## Misconceptions avoided
- Did not call the turbo "free power": the turbine creates exhaust backpressure (Garrett).
- Did not quote a share of fuel energy lost in the exhaust (no solid source found in time).
- "Usually" goes through an intercooler: not every turbo engine has one.

## Simplifications on screen
- One cylinder stands for the whole engine; animation slowed; pressure readout (1.0 → 1.8 atm, runaway 2.3) is illustrative.
  Audi's TT RS example reaches 1.2 bar relative (~2.2 atm absolute), so the numbers are in a realistic range.
- Housings drawn as see-through shells; wastegate shown as a flap on an external bypass.
