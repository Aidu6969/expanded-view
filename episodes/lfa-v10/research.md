## Unsure / how resolved
- **Why the tach is digital:** Wikipedia hedges ("allegedly"). The hook therefore says "Legend says a needle
  tachometer couldn't keep up", and the key beat only states the facts (0.6 s, LCD tach) without claiming Lexus's reason.
  The lagging needle in the hook is labelled ILLUSTRATION.
- **Who tuned the exhaust:** Jalopnik (secondhand, via a book by an LFA engineer) says Sango tuned the exhaust and that
  "Yamaha tuned the exhaust" is a legend. Wikipedia credits Yamaha's music division with the exhaust. To stay safe the
  video only credits Yamaha with the **intake** sound (surge tank + ducts), which Yamaha's own release documents. The
  exhaust is not discussed.
- **Firing order:** the real 1LR-GUE firing order is not given in the sources. The animation's crank (five throws at
  144 deg, shared pins) is a physically consistent even-fire layout; no cylinder numbering/firing order is claimed on
  the firing timeline.
- **Rod length** is not published; 140 mm used for the animation. Bore 88 / stroke 79 from Wikipedia.
- Horsepower (552 bhp / 560 PS) is not stated in the video (sources disagree by 1 hp).
- Surge-tank frequency numbers differ between Yamaha (250 Hz at ~3,000 rpm) and Lexus UK (300 Hz up to 4,000 rpm): not used.

## Misconceptions avoided
- Not claiming the sound is electronically piped/synthesized: Yamaha says "There is no electrical or electronic sound
  processing"; the ducts carry real intake sound.
- Not claiming Yamaha designed the exhaust.
